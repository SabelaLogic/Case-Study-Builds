How it works

Six steps. Zero apps to download. Nothing to explain twice.

01
SaveGuest starts here

Guests get one WhatsApp number on the invite or program — no app to install, no account to create. Straight into a conversation on their own phone.

02
AskAny question, any time

Dress code, directions, timing, dietary needs, gift questions — asked in plain language, any time before or during the day.

03
MatchOmniSync · AI routing

Built on the same routing engine behind our lead-intake work, matched instantly against ENVIE's real event details for this couple.

04
EscalateHuman handoff

Anything outside its confidence, or from a visibly stressed guest, routes straight to a real person on the day-of team — nothing gets guessed.

05
ConfirmENVIE branded

Every answer comes back under ENVIE's own name and voice — not "some concierge bot," a message that feels like it came from the couple's own team.

06
TrackPrivate dashboard

Every question, answered or escalated, logged live for the ops team — visible only to ENVIE, right up until the day is done.
